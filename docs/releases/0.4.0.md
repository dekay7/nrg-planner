# NRG Planner 0.4.0

This release includes the hourly Home Energy dashboard and the ComEd DST/privacy
fixes merged since 0.3.0. Open feature and dependency pull requests are not included.

## What's new

- **An hourly home energy plan.** Home Energy now shows the next 24 hours of
  household demand, solar production, battery supply and exports, alongside a
  separate EV/battery charging and total grid-import graph. Independent linear
  kWh scales keep charging peaks from flattening smaller household flows without
  clipping peaks or stacking overlapping totals.
- **Accessible hourly explanations.** Expandable summaries retain exact action
  periods, EV charging times/current, solar/grid charging contributions and modeled
  ending battery SOC. Times follow Home Assistant's timezone, including repeated
  DST hours. Missing inputs stay unknown rather than becoming zero; fallback and
  conservative outlooks remain labeled.
- **Clearer dashboard organization.** The price and suggested appliance-window
  chart moves to **Why?**. Tooltips wrap within the chart width on small screens
  while retaining dates and UTC offsets. These displays are read-only: selected
  actions, advice, control-price eligibility and cost calculations are unchanged.

## Bug fixes

- **Preserve usable ComEd forecasts on DST days.** One ambiguous or nonexistent
  local hour-ending label no longer discards the entire published day. Valid,
  unambiguous hours are retained, and source health explains omitted labels.
  Missing intervals are not guessed, copied or assigned zero; live-price,
  publication-window, fallback and equipment-control safeguards remain in place.
- **Recognize GitHub-committed Dependabot metadata.** Privacy/history validation
  accepts GitHub's committer identity for legitimate Dependabot commits while
  retaining credential and private-identifier detection.

## Compatibility and upgrade

- Requires **Home Assistant 2026.10.2 or newer**; the tested baseline remains
  2026.10.2 / Python 3.14. The optional graphs target **ApexCharts Card 2.2.3**,
  installed separately through HACS. No runtime or frontend dependency is added.
- No manual storage migration is required from 0.3.0. Settings, explicit control
  permissions, completed learning, measured and forecast-priced cost ledgers,
  saved fallback requests/outage latches and ComEd continuity timestamps are
  retained, subject to normal retention/source-change rules.
- New installations still start in **Observation**, with equipment controls and
  notifications disabled. Upgrades do not enable new permissions or change the
  chosen fallback/ComEd estimate policies. No new setting is required.
- Back up Home Assistant, update through HACS or your existing
  installation method, then **restart Home Assistant**. Manual installers should
  replace only `custom_components/nrg/`. Reload the dashboard/browser after the
  restart so the sensor and graph definitions are updated together.
- Bundled YAML dashboards receive the new layout. If you maintain a separate
  customized dashboard, review and merge the bundled changes yourself; keep
  customizations outside the integration directory.
- The graphs are modeled interval energy, not measured live power or guaranteed
  savings. The live Sankey still uses Home Assistant Energy's configured meters.

## Validation and known findings

- **655 automated tests pass** on Python 3.14.8 / Home Assistant 2026.10.2 with
  Node.js 22.23.4. Coverage includes native fresh setup, invented 0.2.0/0.3.0 store
  restoration in Observation and Control, executable charts, DST forecasts,
  accounting, guarded controls and documentation.
- Privacy/history, Ruff F checks, dependency consistency, whitespace and
  deterministic UI/handbook/dashboard/brand generation pass locally. Exact-commit
  hosted tests/privacy, asset reproducibility, Hassfest and HACS results are linked
  from the GitHub release and [release-preparation PR #25](https://github.com/dekay7/nrg-planner/pull/25).
- **The dependency audit still fails: 40 known advisories** in HA-pinned
  **Pillow 12.3.0** (39) and **uv 0.12.23** (one). Reported fixes remain Pillow
  13.0.0 and uv 0.13.4. No overrides or advisory exclusions are added; this is not
  a fully green validation.
- Automated tests and standalone Chromium checks do not establish an installed-HA
  desktop/mobile walkthrough or verified physical-equipment response. No such
  independent live verification is claimed for this release. New displays remain
  read-only and existing equipment permissions and guards are unchanged.
